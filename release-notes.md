Adds textured brushes, multi-layer frame selection and opening projects from Finder.

### New
- **Textured brushes**: pick from six built-in stamp brushes with paper grain, or import and export your own as .rustbrush files. [Drawing tools](https://rusting-animation.org/docs/drawing-tools)
- **Animation length handle**: drag the end of the timeline to make the animation longer or shorter. [Timeline & layers](https://rusting-animation.org/docs/timeline-and-layers)
- **Open from Finder**: double-click a .rusting file, or drop it on the Dock icon, to open it, and project files now show the Rusting icon.

### Improved
- **Frame selection across layers**: drag across frame cells to select a block over several layers, or Shift+click to select from the playhead, then delete or move only those frames. [Timeline & layers](https://rusting-animation.org/docs/timeline-and-layers)
- **Per-layer frame editing**: Duplicate Frame and Insert Frame now act on the active layer only and keep the animation length.
- **Draw order**: the "In front" option is gone, since it behaved the same as Normal; tools now offer Behind and Normal.

### Fixed
- **Panel sizes at launch**: panels restored too small, such as a timeline that hid its layer rows, grow back to their default size.
- **Fill and colouring**: fixed issues with filling and colouring shapes.
- **Toolbar**: fixed a toolbar display issue.
