Adds a Brush Editor, pen pressure, and copy and paste on the canvas.

### New
- **Brush Editor**: change a brush's tip, paper grain and stamp settings in a window with a live scratchpad.
- **Create Brush from Selection**: turn strokes, fills or pictures you drew into the tip of a new brush.
- **Pen pressure**: on Windows and macOS, the Brush draws thinner and lighter where you press lighter with a drawing tablet pen.
- **Copy and paste**: copy, cut and paste strokes, fills and pictures in place, onto another frame or layer.

### Improved
- **Smoother playback**: upcoming frames are prepared ahead of the playhead, so playback no longer stutters on a new drawing.
- **Paint bucket at the canvas edge**: the canvas edge closes an area like a stroke, so lines running off the canvas bound a fill.

### Fixed
- **Textured brush overpainting**: a stroke drawn over another now fills the paper grain's white dips instead of leaving them.
- **Toolbar blinking**: the tool icons no longer blink while you draw.
