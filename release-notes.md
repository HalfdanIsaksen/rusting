Adds layer groups, layer opacity, GIF export, same-colour merging and a signed macOS app you can download.

### New
- **Layer groups**: put layers in a folder with its own show/hide, lock and opacity, fold it, and drag it to move all its layers at once. [Timeline and layers](https://rusting-animation.org/docs/timeline-and-layers)
- **Layer opacity**: set how see-through each layer is, from 0 to 100%, by dragging or typing in the layer row. [Timeline and layers](https://rusting-animation.org/docs/timeline-and-layers)
- **GIF export**: export the frame range as a looping animated GIF at the document frame rate, for animations up to 15 seconds. [Files and export](https://rusting-animation.org/docs/files-and-export)
- **Merge same colour**: a new stroke or fill that touches fills of the same colour becomes one fill with them, so the shape selects, moves and erases as one item. [Drawing tools](https://rusting-animation.org/docs/drawing-tools)
- **macOS app**: download Rusting as a signed and notarized app in a DMG, and drag it to Applications. [Download](https://rusting-animation.org/download)

### Improved
- **Draw behind fills**: Behind now puts new strokes and fills under existing fills too, not only under strokes. [Drawing tools](https://rusting-animation.org/docs/drawing-tools)

### Fixed
- **Fill shapes**: fills no longer show stray wedges or missing slivers, including after erasing through them.
- **Jumping strokes**: fast drawing no longer makes a stroke jump when the system delivers input in batches.
