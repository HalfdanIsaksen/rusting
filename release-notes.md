Adds a library, reusable animated objects with motion tweens, audio layers, dockable panels, picture import and a text tool.

### New
- **Library**: a panel that lists every imported picture and sound and every object, so you can drag them back onto the canvas or the timeline, rename them or delete the ones you no longer use.
- **Objects**: turn layers and frames, or items selected on the canvas, into an object that plays its own animation wherever you place it, looping or playing once.
- **Motion tweens**: make an object glide between two of its keys, blending position, scale, rotation and opacity with linear, ease in, ease out or ease in-out timing.
- **Audio layers**: import MP3 or WAV sounds onto their own layers, see their waveform on the timeline and hear them in sync while playing or scrubbing (exported videos have no sound yet).
- **Dockable panels**: drag the tool strip, Tool Settings, Library and timeline by their tabs to arrange them around the canvas, and the layout is kept between launches.
- **Picture import**: File → Import… places PNG and JPEG pictures on the canvas, where they move, scale, rotate and export like anything you draw.
- **Text tool**: click the canvas and type, and the text becomes vector shapes that select, transform, erase and export like any fill.

### Improved
- **Pictures stored once**: a picture placed many times is saved only once in the project file, which keeps projects small.
- **Fills join their outline**: a new fill takes in the touching strokes of its colour, so fill and outline become one shape with no seam.

### Fixed
- **Arrow symbols**: arrows in menus, tooltips and the shortcut list, such as in "File → Import…", no longer show as empty boxes.
