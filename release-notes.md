Adds draw order for brushes, shapes and fills, an eraser that works on fills, an eyedropper, and the Rusting logo as the app icon.

### New
- **Draw order**: brush, rectangle, ellipse and paint bucket can draw behind, normally, or in front of everything else in the frame. [Drawing tools](https://rusting-animation.org/docs/drawing-tools)
- **Eraser on fills**: whole mode removes fills it touches; partial mode cuts holes in fills or splits them, keeping their colour and gradient. [Drawing tools](https://rusting-animation.org/docs/drawing-tools)
- **Eyedropper**: pick a colour from the canvas. [Drawing tools](https://rusting-animation.org/docs/drawing-tools)

### Improved
- **App icon**: the Rusting logo is now the window, taskbar and Dock icon, and appears on the start menu. [Start menu](https://rusting-animation.org/docs/start-menu)
